British Values

Welcome

Welcome to the City of Westminster. We are proud to celebrate and promote the core British values of democracy, the rule of law, individual liberty, and mutual respect and tolerance for those of all faiths and backgrounds.

Our Mission

Our mission is to strengthen our community by upholding British values, encouraging active citizenship, and creating a safe, inclusive environment where everyone feels respected and heard.

Key Values and Principles

  • Inclusivity and Community Engagement
  • Transparency and Integrity
  • Sustainability and Environmental Responsibility
  • Health and Well-being
