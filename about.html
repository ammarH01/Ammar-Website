About Westminster

Who We Are

Westminster is one of the most historic and culturally significant areas in the United Kingdom. As a central borough of London, it is home to iconic landmarks, diverse communities, and a rich political heritage.

Our Council

Westminster City Council serves the local community by providing essential services, supporting residents, and maintaining the borough’s infrastructure. The council works to ensure fairness, sustainability, and opportunity for all.

Leadership

Our Vision

We aim to build a fairer, greener, and more inclusive Westminster through community engagement, environmental responsibility, and transparent governance.
